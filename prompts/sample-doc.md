# Project: Stickman Documentary

Bộ prompts cho video "They Built Their Own Coffins"

---

## Scene 1-3: Opening

1. A lone stickman figure standing at the edge of an infinite digital void, cold blue backlighting casting long shadows, minimalist cinematic composition, dark atmospheric mood
2. Close-up of a stickman's hand reaching toward a glowing screen, the light reflecting off an otherwise pitch-black room, eerie blue tones, symbolic isolation
3. Wide shot of an empty corporate office at night, a single stickman sitting at a desk surrounded by thousands of monitors, each displaying code, cold sterile lighting

## Scene 4-6: The Machine

4. A massive industrial machine shaped like a coffin, gears and pipes extending into darkness, a tiny stickman standing before it looking up, dramatic low angle, fog rolling across the floor
5. Binary code raining down like a waterfall onto a stickman who stands unflinching beneath it, neon blue digital rain, cyberpunk aesthetic, dramatic backlighting
6. A stickman training its replacement — a translucent holographic copy of itself — the original fading while the copy grows brighter, symbolic visual metaphor, cold blue palette
